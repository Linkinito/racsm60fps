## Working dependency model

`outer frequency × inner iterations × per-iteration change` is a useful hypothesis for additive paths, not a law for collision, acceleration, damping, thresholds or animation transitions. Under that simplified model, doubling frequency and halving iterations preserves fixed per-iteration work; halving delta as well may require a local 2× input. The actual Pokitaru patch does contain such local doubling. Whether it accounts for movement/jumps/animation must be measured, especially because the loop is conditional and several calls lie outside it.

For exponential interpolation, two half-steps need a compositional coefficient (for example 1−sqrt(1−alpha) in a simple first-order recurrence). This algebra does not validate the Metalis recurrence or cross-level transfer. Acceleration, velocity and position can require different factors.
