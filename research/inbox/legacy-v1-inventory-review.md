# Critical review of the legacy V1 inventory

## Scope and method

This reviews `research/inbox/legacy-v1-inventory.md` against the preserved V1 tree on `v2-research`. It follows `AGENTS.md`, `MIGRATION.md`, and `docs/methodology/EVIDENCE_LEVELS.md`. No game, emulator, generator, patch, or historical file was run or changed. Checks were read-only: file existence, selected source and log inspection, CSV/JSON counts, and ZIP entry listings. The review is selective rather than a word-by-word audit of every V1 artifact; unexamined claims remain unverified, not false.

**Path convention:** `L/` means `01-Travail-et-profil-PPSSPP/Overcompensated-Reprise-2026-09-14/`. `P45/` means `L/sources-v0.4.5/Size_Matters_wrapper_profiler_v0.4.5-prealpha_UCES00420/`. `P50/` means `L/sources-v0.5.0/Size_Matters_wrapper_dispatcher_v0.5.0-prealpha_UCES00420/`. Statuses below apply to the *specific claim stated*, not to every conclusion in its source document. `TESTED` is reserved for the documented scope of a controlled or deterministic check; historical summaries alone are not independent replications.

## Unsupported or overstated claims

1. **Wrong source attribution for the v0.6.1 numbers.** Inventory sections 2 and 5 say `L/development-v0.6.1/LIVE_VALIDATION.json` records 167/167 sites and 36/36 redirects. That file has no such arrays or counts; it has a summary and proof paths. The numbers are directly in `L/connection/dev061-integration-1789598570306.json` and are repeated in `L/development-v0.6.1/NEXT_MENU_STEP.md`. The result is credible within its scope, but the inventory points the reader at the wrong primary evidence.
2. **Profiler stage results are less independently inspectable than “matching traces” suggests.** `P45/POKITARU_STAGE02_RESULT.md`, `P45/RYLLUS_STAGE03_RESULT.md`, and `P45/profiler/IN_SITU_ANALYSIS_v0.4.3_STAGE02.json` / `IN_SITU_ANALYSIS_v0.4.4_STAGE03.json` agree on 55/55 and 57/57 hooks, 496,697 and 122,243 hits, and the Ryllus VBlank histogram. Their input trace hashes are recorded in the derived JSON. The corresponding raw stage 02/03 traces and `status(8).log` / `status(9).log` were not located in `P45/` or the examined v0.4.3–v0.4.5 profiler ZIPs. `L/trace-validation/trace_L01_0001.result.txt` is a later detect-only capture with **zero installed hooks and zero hits**, not support for the 496,697-hit stage 02 claim. The inventory correctly warns about this later snapshot, but its “matching traces” phrase and `TESTED` label need the missing primary-trace qualification. Absence from these locations does not prove the captures never existed.
3. **`CORROBORATED` is too strong for the exact provenance of all 54 manual entries.** `L/inventaire-2026-09-17/INVENTAIRE_MEMOIRE.md` states that 54 RVA writes match the compiled table; `L/development-v0.6.1/VALIDATION_SUMMARY.json` reports 54 source words verified; the live verifier checks the final 167-site memory table. These are useful, but the live JSON does not independently link each of the 54 manual experiment rows to a causal gameplay result, and this review did not join every manual row against the generated and binary tables. Preserve the 54-row integration claim as a recorded static assertion plus a separately observed 167-site runtime check.
4. **Minor path errors obscure reproducibility.** The inventory’s instrumentation paragraph places `PROFILER_SPEC.md` next to `P45/profiler/HOOK_DESIGN.md` without specifying its root: the actual file is `P45/PROFILER_SPEC.md`. Its static-dataset paragraph says `module_fingerprints.csv` comes from the same `P45/tables/` directories, but it is `P45/profiler/module_fingerprints.csv`. Other checked CSV paths and counts were correct.
5. **A parser `PASS` and a write/readback are narrower than a gameplay test.** `L/trace-validation/trace_L03_0004.result.txt` and `trace_L03_0005.result.txt` both say 87,570 hits because they represent cumulative captures; they are not independent repetitions. `L/connection/wf029-apply-result.json` and `wf029-rollback-result.json` prove recorded policy writes and rollback, but contain no vehicle speed or distance measurements. Any “WF-029 failed as a vehicle fix” claim rests on historical qualitative feedback in `L/CONTINUITE.md` / `L/history/`, not these JSON files alone. The inventory mostly states these limits, but its `TESTED (historical)` wording should remain strictly tied to parser invariants, memory state, or the reported local trial.

## Contradictions found in legacy material

1. **Metalis cave chronology.** `L/inventaire-2026-09-17/INVENTAIRE_MEMOIRE.md` says cave 7 word 34 is not integrated; `L/development-v0.6.2-menu/sources/profiler/src/psp_plugin_runtime.c` already contains a Metalis-only `0x3C043F80 → 0x3C043F00` transformation. The older statement is `SUPERSEDED` for v0.6.2, but the later v0.6.3 generated-template/guard conflict is a separate source defect. `L/research-2026-09-18-global/AUDIT_HISTORIQUE.md` describes both; the code independently supports the distinction.
2. **Generalization values.** `L/development-v0.6.3-generalisation/LISEZ_MOI.md` presents the new collection entries as the Metalis correction extended to nine modules. `L/research-2026-09-18-global/audit-v063-pickup.csv` shows 27/45 values differ from the Metalis reference, all with static-only validation. The identity claim is `REJECTED`; which values are correct in other levels is `UNKNOWN`.
3. **Giant Clank crash cause.** The first two interpretations in `L/sessions/plantage-clank-geant-20260917-2134/RAPPORT.md` progress from a v0.6.3 cave suspicion to a LEVEL_15 write diagnosis; the appended third trial contradicts the latter. A later disabled-plugin configuration and `L/research-2026-09-18-global/vanilla-ppsspp.log` establish at most a boot/log context, not a documented replay through the crash point without the plugin. Keep the cause `UNKNOWN`.
4. **Version “current” statements.** `L/ETAT_ACTUEL.md` and earlier sections of `L/START_HERE.md` describe a 17 September installed state; `L/research-2026-09-18-global/RETRAIT_MENU.md` records v0.6.4 installed but disabled on 18 September. These are time-separated states, not evidence that both binaries were active together. Resolve by dated hashes and deployment reports.
5. **Kalidon hit totals need capture identity.** `L/research-2026-09-18-global/AUDIT_HISTORIQUE.md` mentions 67,905 hits for a Kalidon historical passage, whereas `L/CONTINUITE.md` and `L/trace-validation/trace_L03_0004.result.txt` / `0005.result.txt` describe 87,570 for passage B. They may refer to different runs or windows; V1 does not reconcile them at the cited locations. Do not silently substitute one total for the other or add cumulative exports.
6. **Menu static analysis revised its own extraction.** `L/research-2026-09-16/menus/RAPPORT.md` says the early `cycle-data.py` interpreted color words as float NaNs and supersedes that output with `finalize.py` results. The inventory cites the final pause-cycle offsets correctly but does not warn that the earlier extractor's float interpretation is `SUPERSEDED`.

## Important omissions

- **Named timing/control globals and their lifetimes.** `L/development-v0.6.1/sources/profiler/src/psp_plugin_runtime.c` defines `g_rcsm_requested_fps`, `g_rcsm_applied_fps`, and `g_rcsm_fps_pending`; `L/development-v0.6.1/README.md` explains deferred module-load application. The first inventory mentions their state machine but omits these concrete global names and the runtime capture fields in `L/connection/dev061-integration-1789598570306.json`. They are plugin globals, not established game globals; exact addresses are build/session dependent.
- **Unmatched transfer denominator.** The inventory notes 20 Metalis signature transfers but does not count `L/research-2026-09-18-global/metalis_transfer_gaps.csv`: 176 rows say `absent_or_not_unique; no transfer`. This is crucial when evaluating how general the 20 positive matches are.
- **A narrow quantitative timing lead.** `L/CONTINUITE.md` records WF-058 / MungoDrone at about 6.10 s in passage A and 3.05 s in B, and explicitly proposes a targeted investigation. `L/OVERCOMPENSATED_VUE_ENSEMBLE.md` warns these are historical reported values. They deserve inventory as `OBSERVED` reported measurements, with raw video/capture and test control `UNKNOWN`.
- **Save-format negative finding beyond the large null region.** `L/research-2026-09-16/saves/README.md` also supersedes an earlier proposal that skill-point bits 25–47 were a contiguous free flag interval; later material describes nibble groups with gaps. This is relevant to future engine/decompilation work and should not be rediscovered as a fresh free-space claim.
- **Validation artifacts by version.** `L/development-v0.6.1/VALIDATION_SUMMARY.json`, `L/development-v0.6.3-generalisation/VALIDATION_PRINCIALE_V063.json`, and `L/development-v0.6.4-no-menu/{METALIS_CAVE_GUARD_VALIDATION.json,sources/PSP_BUILD_VALIDATION.json}` distinguish host/static/build checks from in-game behavior. The first inventory lists broad tooling but provides too little of this validation chain to assess its `TESTED` labels quickly.
- **Specific menu runtime hazard.** `L/development-v0.6.1/NEXT_MENU_STEP.md` records that an inactive screen-17 object remained in RAM while screen 3 was active, so a first probe read the stale object before correction. This matters for future menu/global-pointer interpretation; object presence is not current ownership.

## Recommended migration priorities

These are review priorities only; no migration is performed here.

1. Preserve immutable anchors first: archive and PRX hashes, module/RVA rules, wrapper catalogs, `P45/profiler/module_fingerprints.csv`, `P45/tables/profiler_ra_lookup.csv`, and the LEVEL_02 discrepancy with its `UNKNOWN` provenance.
2. Preserve raw or derived trace provenance distinctly, starting with profiler stage 02/03 source hashes and the separate `L/trace-validation/` captures. Carry a missing-raw-evidence flag wherever the original capture cannot be found.
3. Preserve version-specific patch ledgers, the 54-row manual join as an unresolved verification task, the v0.6.3 cave conflict, the 27/45 collection mismatch, and v0.6.4's narrow host validation. Do not migrate historical gameplay success labels with table words.
4. Preserve negative results and open hypotheses: Giant Clank crash reversal, WF-029 local rollback, the save-region and skill-bit exclusions, menu extractor correction, and the 176 failed/ambiguous static transfers.
5. Defer authoritative game function/global names and 60 FPS gameplay claims until code, runtime observations and controlled 30/60 measurements can be linked. `L/research-2026-09-16/{menus,render,saves}/`, `L/research-2026-09-18-global/static-inventory/`, and the symbol-bearing `straddle.junk` work are promising decompilation sources but still need rebasing and semantic checks.

## Reviewer conclusion

The first inventory is a **useful navigation map**: its version sequence, major dataset sizes, cave/pickup contradictions, and caution against treating static candidates as patches largely survive spot checks. It is **not a validation ledger**. The most consequential corrections are the wrong v0.6.1 primary citation, the absence of independently inspectable original profiler stage traces in the locations checked, the overbroad `CORROBORATED` label for all 54 manual entries, and the need to keep host/static/runtime-memory tests separate from gameplay tests. The Giant Clank root cause and whole-game 30/60 behavior remain unresolved. Subsequent V2 work should use the inventory to find evidence, then re-establish each technical claim from its exact version, input files, capture and test scope.
